# OpenBastion Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OpenBastion is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone who participates in the project, regardless of experience, identity, background, nationality, disability, appearance, or personal beliefs.

We want our community to focus on building secure, useful, and trustworthy open-source software. Participation in the community is conditional on treating others with dignity and respect.

## Expected behaviour

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and curiosity.
- Respect privacy and obtain consent before sharing another person's personal information.
- Keep security discussions responsible and avoid exposing sensitive details.
- Help maintain an environment where people can ask questions and learn.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or unwanted personal attention.
- Discriminatory, degrading, or insulting language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery in inappropriate community contexts.
- Encouraging violence, doxxing, or other harmful conduct.
- Deliberately exposing credentials, private keys, personal data, or vulnerability details that could put people or systems at risk.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider harmful or abusive in a project community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to OpenBastion repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, chat spaces, project events, and other spaces where a person is representing or participating in the OpenBastion community.

It also applies when community participation moves into public spaces and a person is clearly acting on behalf of OpenBastion.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the OpenBastion organization maintainers privately through GitHub. Do not use a public issue to disclose sensitive personal information.

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and do not publish exploit details.

Please include relevant context and links where safe to do so. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. The maintainers will review reports fairly and may ask for additional information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. Depending on the circumstances, responses may include a warning, a request to stop the behaviour, temporary restrictions, removal of content, exclusion from a community space, or a permanent ban from project spaces.

Actions will be proportionate to the severity and pattern of the behaviour. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation immediately when necessary to protect community members or project security.

Maintainers who are directly involved in a report should not be the sole decision-maker for that report where another suitable maintainer is available.

## No retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith, helps investigate a report, or participates in an enforcement decision is not acceptable.

## Attribution
